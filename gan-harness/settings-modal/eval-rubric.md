# Settings Modal Evaluation Rubric

Score each category from 0 to 10 and calculate the weighted total.

### Design Quality (weight: 0.35)

Hierarchy, composition, spacing, typography, modal presence, clarity, and coherence with Tro's visual language.

### Originality (weight: 0.30)

Distinctive Tro-specific interpretation of the reference rather than a literal clone; memorable navigation and surface details used with restraint.

### Craft (weight: 0.25)

Responsive behavior, polished states, accessible interaction, robust CSS, exact alignment, and thoughtful micro-details.

### Functionality (weight: 0.10)

Existing controls remain usable, modal dismissal works, settings are clearly separated, and no core behavior regresses.

## Pass threshold

Weighted score of 7.5 or higher, with no category below 6.5 and no unresolved accessibility or renderer-sandbox issue.
